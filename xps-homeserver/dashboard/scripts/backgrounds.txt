# Aerial clips behind the dashboard: <scene> <Mixkit video id> <what it shows>
#
# A small pool per scene (morgen, tag, abend, spaet). The wall plays two
# clips of a scene each day and the next two the day after, so over the days
# it walks through the whole list (web/js/scene.js). Every clip here exists
# at 1920×1080 in landscape on Mixkit, so nothing is upscaled or cut to a
# strip. Only calm drone flights: a frame two and three seconds in barely
# differ (checked for each); time-lapses with racing clouds or light trails,
# which only long exposures leave, stay out, whatever their title says.
# fetch-backgrounds.sh slows them further.
#
# Free under the Mixkit Stock Video Free License (https://mixkit.co/license/),
# which allows using them but not redistributing the files, so they are
# downloaded instead of committed: dashboard/scripts/fetch-backgrounds.sh.
# Remove a line and the next run deletes the clip; add one and it is fetched.
